Workers Compensation Insurance

Financial protection for employees injured at work, and for the business that employs them. Cover medical bills and lost wages without the paperwork.

💼 Wage support during recovery
⚡ Faster, surveyor-verified claims

What's covered

  • Hospital and treatment costs after a workplace injury
  • Part of lost wages while an employee recovers
  • Disability benefits, partial or permanent
  • Death benefit for the family
  • Occupational illness linked to the job
  • Rehabilitation and follow-up care

What's not covered

  • Injuries caused by intoxication
  • Self-inflicted injuries
  • Injuries outside work duties
  • Claims made on a lapsed policy
